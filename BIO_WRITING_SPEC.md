# Bio Writing Specification — STEM Achievements

This package follows the shared bio writing spec from `test-data-factory`. See
[`BIO_WRITING_SPEC.md`](https://github.com/mosaic-code-coop/test-data-api/blob/main/BIO_WRITING_SPEC.md)
in that repo for the generic guidance (Should Be / Should NOT Be / Tone).

The sections below are package-specific addenda.

## Geographic and Cultural Sensitivity

- Respect the cultural context of their scientific work
- Acknowledge traditional knowledge systems when relevant
- Use appropriate geographic identifiers
- Recognize the global nature of scientific collaboration
- Avoid Eurocentric perspectives on scientific progress

## Subject-Specific Tone

- **Honor innovation** — recognize scientific creativity and persistence
- **Acknowledge barriers** — gender, racial, and class barriers in STEM are
  real and continuing
- **Don't conflate first-of-X with sole contributor** — collaborative
  attribution matters in science
